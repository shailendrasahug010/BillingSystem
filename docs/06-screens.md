# F. Screen-by-screen specification

Each screen lists who uses it, what it shows, what it lets the user do, the rules it enforces and how it adapts to a phone. **Built** screens exist in the prototype (`apps/web`); the others are specified here and scheduled in [11-roadmap.md](11-roadmap.md). All screens share the shell, components and states from [05-design-system.md](05-design-system.md): skeleton while loading, an empty state that says what to do, an error state with retry, and permission-based hiding of actions.

---

## 1. Login — **Built**
* **Users:** everyone.
* **Layout:** brand panel (desktop only) + form. Language toggle before sign-in.
* **Fields:** mobile number or email, password, "keep me signed in on this device"; *Sign in with OTP instead*; *Forgot password*.
* **After login:** if the user belongs to more than one business, a business chooser (legal name + GSTIN); otherwise straight to the dashboard (or to the last screen used).
* **Rules:** 5 failed attempts → temporary lock + OTP; new device → OTP confirmation; admin roles can require TOTP.
* **Phone:** single column, 48 px fields, numeric keyboard for mobile/OTP.

## 2. Dashboard — **Built**
* **Users:** every role; each sees its own home. With `dashboard.view` (owner, manager, accountant, sales) the sales dashboard below; financial cards need `dashboard.view_financials`, and each card needs its own right too (orders `order.view`, packing and dispatch counts `packing.view` / `dispatch.view`, dues and aging `customer.view_ledger`, stock `product.view`).
* **Today's work (every role, from `GET /home`):** *Promises due today* (customers who promised to pay by today and have not paid since, one-tap call; `customer.view_ledger`), *Staff today* (present, absent, on leave, half day, not marked, "Mark attendance"; `employee.view`), *Leave to decide* (`leave.view`), *Salary for the month* (not worked out / draft / finalised / paid; `payroll.view`). Roles without the sales dashboard also get *Pack next* and *Ready to send* (oldest orders first; `packing.view` / `dispatch.view`), *Expenses this month* (`expense.view`) and *Stock alerts*.
* **Banner for roles without the sales dashboard:** their own figures instead of today's sales: orders to pack and to dispatch (warehouse), present today and leave to decide (HR), to collect and promises due (collectors). Their quick actions are their work: pack orders, send out, stock adjustment, mark attendance, add employee, leave request, collect dues.
* **Top:** date and greeting; **quick actions** row (Create Invoice, New Order, Receive Payment, Purchase Entry, Add Customer, Add Supplier, Pay Supplier, Stock Adjustment, Add Product, Add Expense) with shortcut hints.
* **Today:** Sales, Collection, Purchase, Expenses, Orders — each with the comparison to yesterday.
* **Trend:** sales and collection lines, Daily (30 days) / Weekly (12 weeks) / Monthly / Yearly (financial years), hover crosshair, table view.
* **Money:** outstanding receivable (with overdue amount), outstanding payable. **Orders & warehouse:** pending, to pack, to dispatch (each opens its queue).
* **Range section** (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month, Last month, This FY, custom): total sales with change vs the previous period; top-selling products, top customers, company-wise sales.
* **Bottom:** outstanding by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days past due), **Needs attention** (customers with most overdue, one-tap call), **Stock alerts** (out of stock and low stock).
* **This month:** monthly sales, collection, purchase vs the same days last month.
* **Phone:** quick actions scroll horizontally; tiles two per row; charts full width.

## 3. Customer list — **Built**
* **Search:** name, business name, mobile, GSTIN, city, code (instant, forgiving).
* **Filters:** B2B/B2C, route (beat), has outstanding, overdue; sort by outstanding (default), last purchase, name.
* **Columns:** business + contact + city + mobile, GSTIN (or B2C / Export badge), route, last purchase (date + days ago), payment behaviour badge, outstanding (overdue in red).
* **Actions:** Add customer (short form: type — B2B, B2C, **SEZ** unit with its GSTIN, or **Export** for a buyer abroad, who has no state or PIN code and is billed with place of supply 96 — business name, contact, mobile, GSTIN with checksum and state auto-fill, state, pincode, credit limit), export, row → profile.
* **Phone:** card list with business, city · route, outstanding and behaviour badge.

## 4. Customer profile — **Built**
* **Header:** business name, B2B/B2C, category, contact, address and state, GSTIN, route, sales rep, payment terms, selling price list. Actions: Call, WhatsApp, **Edit** (name, type, mobile, GSTIN, state, pincode, address, city, credit limit, price list; only what changed is saved, checked against the version this device saw; offline it is kept on the device and sent later), Receive payment, New order, **New invoice** (opens the invoice with this customer selected). Receive payment, New order and Add customer work offline too (see 10-offline-sync).
* **Tiles:** total sales (+ invoice count), outstanding (+ overdue), average order value, last purchase, payment behaviour (average days to pay).
* **Tabs:** *Overview* (most purchased products, reorder suggestions table) · *Invoices* (number, date, due date, total, balance, status) · *Payments* · *Ledger* (running balance statement, debit/credit, export PDF/Excel) · *Products* (every product bought: orders, total qty, last purchase, typical interval) · *Credit/debit notes* (Phase 3).
* **Rules:** financial tabs require `customer.view_ledger`.

## 5. Supplier list — **Built** (live)

* Same pattern as customers: search (name, company, mobile, GSTIN), filters (has payable, due this week), columns (supplier, GSTIN, payable, due date of oldest bill, last purchase), add supplier (bank details stored encrypted, only last 4 digits shown).

## 6. Supplier profile — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** header (GSTIN, terms, masked bank/UPI), tiles (payable, total purchases, last purchase, last payment), tabs purchases / payments / ledger, actions Edit, Pay supplier, New purchase bill. *Products purchased* tab: Phase 4.

## 7. Product list — **Built**
* **Search:** name, local name, aliases, code, barcode, brand. **Filters:** company/brand, stock level (all / low / out), category (Phase 3).
* **Columns:** product (code, pack, barcode), brand, HSN, GST (or *Nil rated*), MRP, selling price, margin (needs `product.view_cost`), current stock with level badge. Header shows total stock value.
* **Actions:** Add product, bulk import (Excel with row-level errors), export.

## 8. Product profile — Phase 3/4 (editor **Built**)
* **Built (live):** add / edit dialog with names, aliases, brand and category (created inline), HSN, GST category, barcodes, stock unit, case / box unit and an inner pack (a strip or packet inside the box, e.g. box of 100 = 10 strips of 10; units include PCS, NOS, BOX, CTN, PAC, STRIP, BTL, DOZ, BAG, KG, G, L, ML), minimum / reorder quantity, opening stock and cost, batch / expiry tracking, purchase / selling (= Price A) / MRP and a price per rate list (Price B, C…), and optionally a box / strip price per list (blank = the list's piece price × the pack); only changed fields are sent, and price fields are locked for roles without `product.update_price`.
* **Master data:** name + local name + aliases, code, SKU, barcodes (several), HSN, tax category (with the rate in force today and its history), brand, category/subcategory, base unit and alternate units (1 BOX = 24 PCS), pack size, prices per rate list and unit, MRP, minimum stock / reorder quantity, preferred supplier, batch/expiry/serial tracking, variants, images, active.
* **Analytics tab (Phase 4):** quantity and value sold and purchased, current stock by godown and batch (with expiry), margin, average selling and purchase price, top customers, sales and purchase trend, date filter.
* **Stock tab:** stock ledger (date · transaction · in · out · balance).

## 9. Create invoice — **Built** (highest priority)
Designed for "customer → items → save" in under 30 seconds with the keyboard or a scanner.

**Desktop layout (two columns):**

| Left (work area) | Right (sticky bill summary) |
|---|---|
| **Customer** search (F3; a shop not found yet is added from the search, *Add “…” as a new customer*, with what was typed filled in, and goes on the bill) → **customer card**: business, B2B/B2C, payment behaviour, contact, GSTIN, outstanding (overdue), credit limit with used/available meter, last purchase (days ago), average order, average days to pay; credit warning banner when the bill would exceed the limit or invoices are overdue | Invoice number (next in series), date, due date (from credit days) |
| **Product search** (F2) with barcode scan: results show name (local name in Hindi UI), code, brand, pack, HSN, GST, price for this customer's rate list, stock badge, "bought 12×" | Supply type and place of supply: *Intra-state · CGST + SGST* (or UTGST) / *Inter-state · IGST*; for an **Export** or **SEZ** customer instead: *Under LUT / bond, no IGST* (the default while the firm's LUT is valid on the bill date) or *With payment of IGST*, the LUT in use (or a warning that there is none), and for an export the shipping bill number, date and port code (optional, can be added on the saved invoice) |
| **Smart suggestions** (tabs: For this customer, Reorder due, Frequently bought, Bought together, Last order): each shows the reason ("Usually every 7 days · last bought 6 days ago"), a "why?" popover with all reasons and batch expiry, stock flags and a one-click **+ suggested qty** button; Alt+1…9 adds the first nine; reorder tab has a table view (product, last qty, last purchase, suggested qty, status) | Lines · qty, subtotal, discount, taxable value, CGST, SGST/UTGST or IGST, cess, round-off, **grand total**, rate-wise tax breakup |
| **Items table:** #, product (code, HSN, pack, batch), qty stepper with the **unit** (the stock unit or one of the product's packs, e.g. PCS / STRIP (10 PCS) / BOX (100 PCS); changing it re-prices the line: the pack's own rate-list price, else the customer's price for one piece times the pack), rate (editable), discount %, GST %, amount (+ tax); warnings inline: *Only 8 in stock*, *Below purchase cost*; billing from the keyboard: Enter in the product search adds the item and puts the cursor in its qty, Enter moves qty → rate → back to the product search (straight back from qty when the role cannot change prices), Tab goes qty → unit → rate → Disc % → back to the product search; F2 jumps to the search at any time | **Amount received** (+ "Fully paid"), payment mode (Cash, UPI, Bank transfer, Cheque, Card), balance or advance |
| | **Save invoice** (Ctrl+Enter), Save & print, Share on WhatsApp; on the saved invoice: Print, **Download PDF** (made on the server, English / Hindi), **WhatsApp** (the PDF file itself on phones), **Send** (from the firm's WhatsApp Business number or email, with the PDF) |

**Rules:** totals come from the shared engine (identical to the server); tax is resolved on the invoice date; the customer's rate list (selling price list: Price A / B / C, shown on the customer card; chosen when the customer is added and changeable on their page; renamed, added and defaulted under Settings → Price lists) prices lines; changing the customer re-prices; products already on the bill disappear from suggestions; negative stock and credit follow the business policy (warn or block); nothing is ever added automatically. After save: toast with the number and a *Details* link; the draft resets for the next customer.

**Phone ("Quick invoice"):** three steps with a progress bar — **1 Customer** (search or recent customers) → **2 Products** (large search, scan button, suggestions with one-tap add, cart with −/+ and quick +case/+10 buttons, sticky total + Next) → **3 Review & pay** (customer card, item review, summary with amount received and payment mode, Save / Save & print / WhatsApp).

## 10. Invoice details — **Built**
* Header: number, customer, total, payment status; actions: print, PDF, share (WhatsApp/email), **print language** (English / हिन्दी), cancel (Phase 3), credit note (Phase 3).
* **E-invoice and e-way bill** (when the bill needs one or has one): *JSON* downloads the bill in the e-invoice portal's (INV-01) or e-way bill portal's format for upload with the firm's own login; *Enter IRN* records the IRN, acknowledgement number and time and the signed QR code the portal returned; *Enter e-way bill no.* its number, date and validity. The printed invoice and PDF then carry the IRN, acknowledgement, QR code and e-way bill. A cancelled bill with an IRN reminds to cancel it on the portal (within 24 hours).
* **Document (A4):** business name, address, GSTIN; *Tax Invoice — Original for recipient*; number, date, due date, place of supply, reverse charge; bill-to and ship-to with GSTIN and state code; lines (description with batch/expiry, HSN, qty + unit, rate, discount, taxable, GST %, CGST + SGST/UTGST or IGST, amount); HSN tax summary; taxable value, taxes, round-off, **grand total**; paid and balance; amount in words (in the print language); bank and UPI details; terms; *For <business> — Authorised signatory*; "computer-generated invoice".
* **Exports and SEZ supplies:** a badge with the type (*Export · under LUT*, *SEZ · with IGST*…); the document carries the declaration the rules require in capitals, the LUT ARN it was made under and the shipping bill; an export shows its **shipping bill** (or that it is not added yet) with *Add / Change shipping bill* (`invoice.update`), allowed after the month is locked since it is not part of the tax.
* Templates: A4, A5, 80 mm thermal; configurable header, terms, logo, bank details, columns.

## 11. Sales order — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** list (open / pending / confirmed / all, search), "Stock needed" (open order quantities against stock), new order (Alt+O, or from the customer profile) with units / cases and free goods, prices from the customer's rate list, order page with status history, confirm / cancel (reason) / packing / dispatch / delivered per the workflow and permissions, make invoice for everything open or chosen quantities at the order's rates, linked invoices. *Suggestions in the order entry, route and salesperson filters:* Phase 4.
* List with status tabs (Draft, Pending, Confirmed, Packing, Packed, Dispatched, Delivered, Cancelled), route and salesperson filters.
* Create: same customer + items experience as the invoice (suggestions included), expected delivery date, source (counter, salesperson app, phone, WhatsApp).
* Detail: timeline of status changes (who, when, note), fulfilment progress bars (packed / dispatched / delivered / invoiced per line), actions allowed by the workflow (`allowedTransitions`), convert to invoice (full or partial).

## 12. Packing — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** queue of confirmed orders (by expected delivery), pack dialog with scan-to-pack (barcode or product name), quantities per line with what is left, batches split first-expiry-first, box numbers, box count, weight and packer; the order moves to packing / packed; packing slips (print, unpack). *Grouping the queue by route, a scanner-first phone layout:* later.
* **Queue:** orders to pack, grouped by route / dispatch date, oldest first.
* **Packing screen:** order number, customer, lines with ordered, packed and remaining qty, batch (suggested first-expiry-first-out) and expiry, weight; scan a barcode to count up; partial packing allowed; box count and weight; *Mark packed*; print packing slip.
* **Phone/scanner:** one line at a time, huge counters, scan-driven.

## 13. Dispatch — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** posted bills not yet on a trip (by route and city), plan a trip with vehicle, driver (employee or name + mobile), transporter, LR and expected date, delivery order, dispatch now or later, per-stop delivered / returned with the receiver's name, loading list, printable trip sheet with amounts to collect; each stop's e-way bill number on the screen and the sheet, and *E-way bills (JSON)* for the trip's bills above the limit that have none yet, with the trip's vehicle, transporter and LR. *Proof-of-delivery photo:* dropped.
* Create a dispatch (vehicle trip): vehicle number, driver (employee or name + mobile), transporter, LR number/date, e-way bill number, dispatch date, expected delivery; add packed orders (by route); reorder delivery sequence.
* Delivery: per order *Delivered / Partly delivered / Returned*, received by, proof of delivery photo, remarks; delivery date recorded; print dispatch note.

## 14. Purchase entry — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** supplier search, bill number and dates, ITC eligibility, reverse charge, lines (search/scan, unit or case, qty, free qty, rate, discount, supplier's GST override, batch/expiry when tracked), freight and other charges, payment at entry, live totals from the shared engine (the server rejects any difference), purchase detail with payments and cancel; returns and suppliers' credit notes, reverse-charge bills included (the value back from the supplier, the GST off the reverse-charge tax). *Duplicate check as you type, landed-cost preview:* later.
* Supplier (with outstanding and last purchase), supplier invoice number and date (duplicate check as you type), received date, godown.
* Lines: product search/scan, qty, free qty, purchase rate, discount, GST (from tax category, editable for supplier's rate), batch, mfg/expiry, MRP; landed cost preview; other charges (freight, loading) apportioned.
* Totals: taxable, CGST/SGST or IGST (from the supplier's state), round-off, total; ITC eligibility; attach bill photo/PDF.
* **Post:** stock in, cost update, supplier balance, inward GST — one transaction. Purchase return and supplier notes from the purchase detail.

## 15. Inventory — **Built** (live, single godown)
* **Built:** stock list with level filter, value and average cost (for roles allowed to see them), CSV export, stock ledger per product, adjustments (physical count posts the difference; damage, expiry, theft, sample, own use) and their history. *Transfers, per-godown/batch views, expiry list:* Phase 4.
* **Stock list:** product, godown, batch, expiry, qty, value (needs `inventory.view_value`), level; filters: brand, category, godown, low/out/expiring.
* **Stock ledger:** per product (and godown/batch): date · transaction (with link to the document) · in · out · balance.
* **Adjustments:** reason (damage, expiry, count correction, theft, sample, internal use), lines with system qty vs counted qty, approval when configured. **Transfers:** from/to godown, in-transit and receive. **Expiry:** batches expiring in N days with suggested action.

## 16. Payment collection — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** receive payment and pay supplier (oldest bills first, editable per bill, remainder kept as advance), cheque number/date/bank and status (deposited, cleared, bounced — a bounce reverses the allocation), apply an advance later, cancel with reason, list with filters and CSV export. *Receipt print/WhatsApp, the offline phone "Collect" flow:* Phase 4/5.
* **Receive payment:** customer (outstanding, overdue list), amount, method; for a part payment, an optional date the customer will pay the rest by (a promise to pay, shown on Outstanding and on the receipt) (Cash, UPI, Bank transfer, Cheque — with cheque number/date/bank, Card), reference (UTR), date, collected by; allocation to open invoices (oldest first by default, editable per invoice), remainder as advance; receipt print/WhatsApp.
* **Pay supplier:** mirror image against open bills.
* **Lists:** receipts and payments with filters (method, date, collected by), cheque status (deposited, cleared, bounced — bounce reverses the allocation and notifies).
* **Phone:** "Collect" flow for salespeople on a beat: customer → amount → method → save (works offline).

## 16a. Outstanding (money to collect) — **Built**
* **For collectors:** everyone who owes money, largest first, with a chip per city (customers, total; "Dewas" and "DEWAS" are one place, customers without a city last). A collector going to one town picks it and sees only that town. Search by name, mobile or code; route; only overdue; sort by amount, overdue, oldest due or name. A banner shows what the chosen place owes: to collect, overdue, not due yet.
* **Desktop:** a table (customer and mobile, city and route, open bills, oldest due with days late, last payment, overdue, to collect) with totals. **Phone:** cards with the amount and a call button at the thumb.
* **A customer** opens in a sheet (a bottom sheet on phones): Call, a WhatsApp reminder with the dues typed in, to collect / overdue / not due / last payment, credit terms, aging, each open bill with its days late (opens the invoice), opening balance, the last five payments, visits and promises; print the customer's dues, open the customer.
* **Collect there:** *Receive payment* starts at what is overdue (editable). When the customer pays only part, the form shows what will be left and asks (optionally) **when they will pay the rest** (tomorrow, in 3 days, next week or any day): saved as their promise to pay (after the payment, so it stays open) and written on the receipt ("Rest ₹570 promised by 5 Oct 2026"). Once saved, the **receipt** opens at once: print (English or Hindi), PDF, WhatsApp, send. Without a connection the payment is kept on the phone and a **provisional receipt** (no number yet) can be printed or sent on WhatsApp; the numbered one comes after the sync.
* **Not paid?** A **promise to pay** (by a date: tomorrow, in 3 days, next week or any day; amount optional; a note) or a **visit note** (shop closed, owner not there, asked to come later, money not ready, dispute, or typed). Kept offline and sent later. The open promise shows on the list and the sheet ("Promised ₹500 by 3 Oct", red once missed) until the customer next pays; the last note shows under the name; *Promise due* lists who promised to pay by today; sort by promise date; the printed list carries the promise.
* **Print** the list for the day's round (firm letterhead, place, a blank *Collected* column, totals, collector's signature). **Download** PDF, Excel or CSV of what is on screen; every download is in the audit log.
* **Who:** `customer.view_ledger` (owner, manager, sales, accountant), which covers downloading this list too; not warehouse or HR. **Offline:** the balances on the device, printable and as CSV; the bills show again once online.

## 21. Business reports — **Built** (live)
* **Built:** sales (day/week/month, customer, product, brand, category, salesperson), collections, purchases, outstanding with aging, profit (margin by product, company, category, customer, salesperson, month), profit & loss (gross profit, expenses, salaries, net profit), products (fast / slow / not moving, stock value, days of cover); Product Reports and Company Reports open the products and company-profit views; preset or custom periods, export as PDF (letterhead, totals, page numbers), Excel (.xlsx with typed numbers and dates, frozen header, totals) or CSV.
* **Built — Insights (Phase 5.5), the first tab for roles with `report.view_bi`:** the period against the period before or the same dates a year earlier (kept in a saved view like the period). Tiles for net sales, gross margin and margin % (change in points), bills, average bill, customers billed, new customers, return rate, collections, what customers owe and days to collect, stock value, stock turnover and days of stock, each with its earlier value and an arrow coloured by whether the change is good (days to collect going up is red). A 12-month sales and margin trend. **Where sales come from**: customers, companies or salespeople with sales, margin, share (bar), ABC class (A: the few making the first 80%), the earlier period, change and status (new, lost, growing, declining), plus a line on new and lost customers and what the lost ones bought. **Fast movers**, **slow movers** (stock lasting over 90 days at the period's pace) and **dead stock** (no sale for 90 days), with the money tied up. * **Built — saved views:** **Save view** keeps the open report's tab, period, grouping and filters under a name, privately or shared with the team (a preset such as "This month" stays this month; a custom range keeps its dates). **Saved views** lists the person's own views, then those the team shared for reports their role opens, each with its report, period and grouping and who shared it. Opening one switches to its tab with its settings; a bar shows the open view, says when it has been changed, and offers Save changes, Rename or share, Delete (with a warning when the team uses it) or, for someone else's view, Save as my view. Moving to another tab closes the view. Works the same on a phone.
* Report catalogue: sales (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly, customer, product, company, category, salesperson), collections (today, yesterday, week, month, year, custom), purchases (supplier, product, company, month, year), outstanding (customer/supplier, aging 0–30/31–60/61–90/90+), expenses, product analytics, company report, BI (gross margin, inventory value and turnover, fast/slow movers, customer and company contribution, period comparison such as Sep 2026 vs Sep 2025).
