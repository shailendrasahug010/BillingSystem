### Phase 3: what was built, and what moved

* **Built as planned:** posting transactions for invoices, purchases and payments (gap-free numbering under concurrency, stock and party ledgers, GST register, audit, outbox rows), cancellations with reversals, GST period lock, credit and negative-stock policies, rate lists, moving-average and landed cost, reverse charge, ITC eligibility, batches and expiry, allocation and advances, cheque bounce, statements, aging, dashboard, reports with CSV export, GST summary (B2B/B2CL/B2CS, HSN), global search, purchase-history suggestions with reasons, English/Hindi messages for every API error.
* **SaaS additions:** self-service sign-up with OTP creates the business with its defaults (roles, series, settings, godown, cash account, starter GST rate buckets from the 22-Sep-2025 structure); users can own or join several businesses; trial/active/suspended status; RLS on the businesses table itself; foreign-key ids checked against the tenant.
* **Web screens:** every Phase 3 API has its screen in the live app (products add/edit, suppliers and statements, purchase bill entry, payments with allocation / advances / cheque status / cancel, inventory with stock ledger and adjustments, sales / collection / purchase / outstanding reports with CSV export, monthly GST summary, users & roles, business profile, rules and number series). Module screens load on first use to keep the first load small. `apps/web/e2e/operations-flow.mjs` drives them end to end.
* **Changed:** Fastify instead of NestJS (see [02-technology-stack.md](02-technology-stack.md#fastify-api)).
* **Added before the pilot** (first planned as Phase 3 leftovers or Phase 4): sales orders with confirmation and full / partial invoicing through the same posting code as invoices, open-order demand against stock; sales and purchase returns as credit / debit notes (stock, ledgers, document balances, GST register with the original invoice, netted in the GST summary); CSV import of products and customers with row-level errors (5,000 products in about two seconds); godowns and stock transfers; printable receipts / vouchers and credit / debit notes, "Save as PDF" from the print dialog, WhatsApp share links; configurable invoice terms; audit log screen.
* **Moved to Phase 4:** pg-boss worker (outbox rows are already written; summaries are computed live today), server-side PDF and the WhatsApp Business API / email adapters (the browser prints the same templates and opens WhatsApp with the message), object storage (MinIO/S3) and bill photos, XLSX files (CSV import is built), packing and dispatch documents (order statuses can already be moved through packing, dispatch and delivery), returns against reverse-charge bills, device registry. The 10,000-invoice randomised client/server property test is replaced for now by: one shared engine, the server rejecting any total difference (`TOTALS_MISMATCH`), and a browser end-to-end run that posts a bill and checks the server total.

### Phase 4: what was built, and what is left

* **4.1 Sales orders** (built before the pilot): workflow steps per business, status history, partial invoicing. Packing and dispatch now move orders through packed, dispatched and delivered.
* **4.2 Packing & dispatch:** packing queue of confirmed orders with what is left per line, scan-to-pack by barcode or name, batches split first-expiry-first, box numbers and weight, printable packing slips, unpack; dispatch of posted bills (with or without an order) as trips with vehicle, driver or employee, transporter and LR, stops in delivery order, planned or leaving now, delivered / partly delivered / returned per stop with the receiver's name, loading list, printable trip sheet with amounts to collect.
* **4.3 Credit/debit notes:** returns (built before the pilot) plus value-only notes: discount after sale and rate difference against an invoice, and the supplier's credit note on a purchase, by percent, amount spread over the lines, or per line; later returns credit only what the goods are still worth.
* **4.4 GST reports & exports:** `gstr1-json@2026.10` (B2B, B2CL, B2CS net of returns, EXP, CDNR, CDNUR for notes on B2CL sales and exports, nil / exempt, HSN split B2B / B2C, document summary with cancelled numbers) with warnings to fix before filing, the offline tool's CSV layout per table, every download recorded with its formatter version; GSTR-3B (3.1, 3.2, 4 with section 17(5) reversal, indicative 6.1 with credit used in the rule 88A order and reverse charge in cash); lock / unlock a month and record the filing ARN. Golden-file tests use a month worked out by hand. **Open:** acceptance by the GST offline tool with a real month (the JSON layout and version string must be confirmed against the tool in use before the first filing).
* **4.5 Employees, leave, payroll:** employee master (bank / PAN / Aadhaar encrypted, full values only with `employee.view_sensitive`, audited), departments, daily attendance with overtime and a month register, leave types / balances / requests with approval, salary structures from a date, advances and loans, monthly payroll from attendance and unpaid leave (calendar-month basis) with manual amounts, recalculation, finalize (recoveries applied), pay (one payment per person), salary slip in English or Hindi; PF, ESI and professional tax worked out by law (added afterwards, see below). **Open:** a 25-employee comparison with a real spreadsheet, and the PT slabs of each state checked against its current notification.
* **4.6 Expenses:** categories (editable, Hindi names), entry with the amount before or including GST, vendor GSTIN for input credit (not claimable for composition / unregistered businesses), cancel, report by category / month / method with CSV.
* **4.7 Advanced reports:** margin by product, company, category, customer, salesperson or month (net of returns and discounts, cost at the time of sale), P&L (gross profit, expenses, salaries of finalized months, net profit), product movement (fast / slow / not moving, stock value, days of cover). Saved report views (private or shared with the team, per report) and PDF export of reports were added afterwards.
* **Added after Phase 4:** supplier bill import (Purchases → Import bill: the supplier's Excel / CSV / PDF bill matched to our products, items we do not have yet added as new products in one step from the bill (GST, unit, HSN, rate, MRP, batch tracking; selling price by margin, never above a per-piece MRP), reviewed in purchase entry, matches remembered per supplier, duplicate bills flagged); Excel (.xlsx) for product and customer imports; the web app is installable on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone (manifest, icons, a service worker that opens the app without a network and updates only when the user reloads); [HOW-TO-RUN.md](../HOW-TO-RUN.md) for running, installing and deploying it; the product is named **Digital Bahi** (logo, tagline, colours, app icon), and each firm has its own identity next to it (uploaded logo, name, address, mobile, email) on screen and on every printed document.
* **PDF import and export:** supplier bills, price lists and customer lists can be imported from PDFs made by billing software (read on the device with pdf.js; scanned PDFs and photos of printed bills and lists are read with Tesseract on the server, see below), including big companies' tax invoices (headings over three lines, each item's name above and below its numbers, "CGST@6 + SGST@6" tax text, a second page without the heading; the purchase keeps the GST printed on the bill, and a rate no longer in the GST list is flagged so today's rate can be set for all new products at once); every report downloads as a PDF with the firm's letterhead, totals and page numbers (made on the server with PDFKit and IBM Plex Sans Devanagari). Photo attachments (bill photos, receipts, proof of delivery) were dropped at the owner's request.
* **Reading photos and scanned bills:** a printed bill can be photographed (Purchases → Import bill → Photo of the bill) or uploaded as a scanned PDF, and product or customer lists the same way. The page is cleaned up on the device (sized so the print is the height the reader handles best, lighting evened, table rules erased, in a worker), read by Tesseract on the server (tesseract.js with the compact English model, nothing stored) and the table rebuilt by the same code as for a PDF made by billing software; purchase entry asks for every line to be checked against the paper. Needs a connection.
* **Offline billing:** the app opens without a network from the copy of the business kept on the device; bills are saved offline with the device's own series (A1/26-27/0001), printed at once, and sent by themselves when the connection is back (idempotent, numbers kept, block rules only warn); device list and removal in Settings. Payments, orders, customer edits, purchases, expenses and returns work offline too; the pull feed and the "needs review" state followed in 5.3 ([10-offline-sync.md](10-offline-sync.md#0-what-is-built-today)).
* **Document PDFs:** tax invoices, bills of supply, credit / debit notes and receipts / payment vouchers are made as PDF files on the server (PDFKit, the firm's letterhead and logo, GST table with CGST / SGST or IGST, HSN summary, totals with payments and returns, amount in words, terms, signatory, page numbers; English or Hindi with Devanagari shaping). **Download PDF** everywhere; on phones **WhatsApp** shares the PDF file itself with the message.
* **WhatsApp Business and email:** each firm's own WhatsApp Business number (Meta Cloud API with approved templates and the PDF as a document header, or any provider through a signed webhook) and email (the platform's mail server or the firm's SMTP); secrets encrypted and write-only; **Send** on invoices, notes and receipts; **Messages** with the log of every send (status, error, retry) and **payment reminders** to customers with overdue bills (queued and sent in the background); new `message.view` / `message.send` permissions; test mode records instead of sending.
* **Excel reports:** every report downloads as an .xlsx workbook (write-excel-file on the server): title, firm and period on top, frozen header row, numbers and dates as real values, totals in bold; text is never written as a formula.
* **Backup & Restore:** the owner downloads the whole business as one file (gzipped JSON, optionally AES-256-GCM with a password), emails a protected copy, sees earlier backups; a file is restored only as a NEW business with every id and reference rewritten from the schema's foreign keys (references outside the file dropped or refused). Scheduled automatic backups per firm were added afterwards.
* **Exports and supplies to SEZ:** a customer is marked **Export** (a buyer abroad, place of supply 96) or **SEZ** (a unit with its GSTIN); their bills are zero-rated, either with payment of IGST or under the firm's letter of undertaking (LUT, saved in Settings with its ARN and last valid day; the default while it is valid) with no tax. The invoice prints the declaration the rules require, the LUT ARN and the export's shipping bill (number, date, port code; given on the bill or added later). GSTR-1 reports exports in EXP (WPAY / WOPAY with the shipping bill), SEZ supplies in B2B (SEWP / SEWOP) and notes on exports in CDNUR; GSTR-3B puts them in 3.1(b) and leaves exports out of 3.2.
* **PF, ESI and professional tax:** turned on per business in Settings; each slip then carries PF (12% of PF wages, up to the ₹15,000 ceiling unless the employer pays on full wages; PF wages under the Code on Social Security, 2020: basic and DA, and at least half the pay; the employer's 12% split into EPS 8.33% capped at ₹1,250 and EPF; members outside the pension scheme), ESI (0.75% / 3.25% rounded up to the rupee, for pay up to ₹21,000 at the start of each April–September / October–March period; no employee share up to ₹176 a day) and professional tax (state slabs on monthly or yearly pay, a different February / March amount, women's slabs; built in for eight states and editable). The run shows the challans (PF accounts 1, 2 with the ₹500 minimum, 10, 21) and downloads the EPFO ECR 2.0 file, the ESIC monthly sheet and the PT list; employees carry UAN, ESI number, gender and PF / EPS membership.
* **E-invoice and e-way bill without a GSP:** e-invoicing on / off and the e-way bill limit in Settings; GST Reports → *E-invoice & e-way bill* lists the month's B2B, SEZ and export bills and credit notes on them with what the portal would refuse, downloads the IRP's bulk JSON (INV-01 v1.1: items with batch, taxed charges as a service line, export and credit-note references) and the e-way bill portal's bulk JSON (Part B from the dispatch trip or typed in); the IRN, acknowledgement and signed QR code and the e-way bill number are entered back and print on the invoice and PDF (QR drawn from the signed text); trips show each bill's e-way bill and download the ones still due. **Open:** the file layouts checked against the portals with a real upload; an adapter to a GSP when the business has one (6.5).
* **Error log:** every server error and every crash in the browser is written to `logs/errors-YYYY-MM-DD.log` (readable) and `.jsonl` with a reference the user sees, the file, line and function (browser stacks mapped back through the build's private source maps), who, which screen and the request; database errors are put in plain words. `pnpm --filter @digitalbahi/api errors` summarises them.
* **Returns against reverse-charge purchases:** purchase returns and suppliers' credit notes on a reverse-charge bill take back only the goods' value from the supplier (they never charged the GST); the note's GST is flagged reverse charge, so GSTR-3B lowers both 3.1(d) and the credit in 4A(3), and cancelling the note puts them back. The printed note shows its GST apart from the total.
* **Still not built (moved to later phases):** native shells (Capacitor for Android device features, Tauri for direct printing on Windows; store listing works today by packaging the installable app, see HOW-TO-RUN.md section 6).

### Phase 5: what was built, and what is left

* **Before 5.1, housekeeping:** the code name is `digitalbahi` (packages `@digitalbahi/*`, database `digitalbahi`, roles `digitalbahi_owner` / `digitalbahi_app`; servers installed before keep their data, see HOW-TO-RUN.md), and every table and column is described in the generated [database reference](12-database-reference.md), which CI keeps up to date.
* **5.1 Purchase-history service (built):** the outbox worker (in the API process, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, retries with backoff; pg-boss was not needed) refreshes a customer's `customer_product_stats` and `customer_stats` after each bill, payment or note of theirs; `/customers/:id/insight` and `/suggestions` read them (worked out live while none is stored or it is over two days old); the nightly job (once per business and day) checks the stored figures against a fresh computation (drift), rebuilds them, mines `product_associations`, checks `customers.balance`, `suppliers.balance` and `stock_balances` against their ledgers and reports to the error log; `GET /customers/:id/reorder` and `GET /reorder/due` (customers to visit, by beat or salesperson); the billing screen sends what it offered and what was taken (`suggestion_feedback`, summary by reason and position). **Acceptance:** stored figures equal a full rebuild (drift 0 in the tests and on a real run; a tampered row is found and rebuilt); `/suggestions` at 7 ms p95 for a customer with 360 bills of 15 lines. **Then added:** returns lower what was bought (a line returned in full is not bought); the nightly job tunes `suggestions.weights` per business from the last 90 days of feedback, judging each kind of suggestion against the take rate of the positions it was shown at (`suggestions.auto_tune`, off once the weights are saved by hand); `sales_daily_product` and `sales_daily_customer` are filled (each day a bill, return, payment or purchase falls on is worked out whole by the outbox worker, every day again at night with drift counted, marked complete in `analytics_runs`), and the sales report by product and the home screen's top products and companies read them for past days. **Acceptance:** `purchase-history.test.ts` (returns, tuning, weights set by hand kept) and `sales-daily.test.ts` (summaries equal to the bills before and after back-dated and cancelled documents; drift found and put right). **With 5.4:** today's beat brings each shop's buying to the phone (`/beats/today?insights=1`), kept there encrypted, so the bill screen suggests products without a connection.
* **5.2 Notifications (built):** `reminder_rules` (nine per business: customer and supplier payments due, low stock, expiring stock, orders to confirm / pack / dispatch, leave requests, salary due; on / off, hour in the business's time zone, days ahead, day of the month); the API's scheduler (`REMINDER_SCHEDULER_SECONDS`, default 300) runs each due rule once a day (claimed by `last_run_on`, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) and leaves one summary notification per condition (dedupe key per day, salary per month); the outbox worker raises notifications as orders move on and leave is asked for and decided; each is addressed to a permission or one person, read and dismissed per person (`notification_receipts`), kept 90 days; `GET /notifications/stream` (server-sent events through PostgreSQL `LISTEN` / `NOTIFY`, ids only) keeps the bell current; the Notifications screen has the inbox and the rules with *Check now*; texts in English and Hindi, with a test that every key the server sends exists with all its values. **Acceptance:** each reminder type fires once per condition at its hour (the API tests run the checks twice, again the next day and with a rule switched off; a check run by hand is the day's one); in the browser flow a new order shows on the owner's bell 0.6 s after it is placed, without a reload; the API test checks the warehouse's stream hears nothing of it. **Then added:** *Orders waiting too long*, an hourly rule (`last_run_at`) that tells those who confirm orders about each order that has waited the set hours (default 4) at one step, once per order; *Payment reminders to customers*, off until the business turns it on, which sends each customer with overdue bills a reminder by WhatsApp, else SMS (or one chosen channel), in their language (`customers.message_locale`; the WhatsApp templates' Hindi language code; SMS DLT template ids per language) on a schedule: the first some days after the oldest unpaid bill fell due, then every few days, at most so many while those bills stay unpaid, above a least overdue amount; one per customer and day (unique index on `messages.reminder_on`), listed under Messages as *Automatic*, one notification a day to the staff with how many went and who could not be reached; the card shows who is due one today and *Send now*. Per-customer opt-out: *No payment reminders* on the customer, or the customer replies **STOP** on WhatsApp (Meta's or the provider's signed callback; START undoes a reply's stop), which also leaves them out of the reminders sent by hand. SMS goes through the business's own gateway (signed webhook, DLT sender id). Migration `0026_reminders_messaging`. **Acceptance:** `auto-reminders.test.ts` (schedule, language and template, opt-out by staff and by STOP / START, one a day when two runs meet, SMS with DLT ids, the hourly orders rule told once per order) and `auto-reminders-flow.mjs` in the browser. **Push to phones:** `push_tokens` (business, person, sign-in, language); `POST/DELETE /push/tokens`, `POST /push/test`; each new notification goes through the outbox worker to the phones of those it is for, in each phone's language, by FCM's HTTP v1 API with a service account (`FCM_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`; `PUSH_DRIVER` log in development), at most once; gone tokens deleted, ended sign-ins stop pushes at once and are cleared nightly. Migration `0027_push_tokens`. **Acceptance:** `push.test.ts` (who gets what, language, once, through an order's steps, an uninstalled app, signing out) and `push-transport.test.ts` (signed sign-in, message format, retry after a refused access token, gone tokens) against a stand-in for Google. The Android app's side (registering, channels, opening a push) comes with 5.4.
* **5.3 Offline-first (built):** what was built before (service worker, encrypted IndexedDB copy, the outbox for bills, payments, orders, customers, purchases, expenses and returns, device series, sync, the sync chip and *Waiting on this device*) plus: bills made offline that broke a rule by the time they arrived are held for review instead of refused (`invoices.review_*`, `invoice.review`, notified, on the home screen and the bill, marked reviewed or cancelled); the pull feed `GET /sync/pull` (change log per transaction, exact snapshot cursors, removed ids, lists by hash, overdue rolled with the days, role-limited) used at start, after a sync, on return online and every 3 minutes; customer changes made offline merged field by field (the later change wins, the loser audited and notified); devices report their waiting work (Settings → Devices; reminder rule *Devices with work not sent*). The chaos test found two server bugs, fixed: two requests with the same Idempotency-Key at once (now serialised) and deadlocks between bills for the same customer (balance locks now `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`). **Acceptance:** `offline-chaos.test.ts`, 3 devices offline for a day, 300 bills + 100 receipts synced at once with lost answers, double sends and drops: no duplicates, unbroken device series, every total the one the device printed, balances, stock, ledgers and GST register agreeing (passes with several seeds); browser flow `offline-sync-flow.mjs`. **Then added:** discount notes and cancelling bills offline (a bill made offline can be cancelled before it was sent: it goes after the bill); `POST /sync/push` (up to 50 items per request, each through its own route with its key, `target` / `after` for work on what an earlier item made); `sync-push.test.ts` and the browser flow `weak-network-flow.mjs` (three phone-sized browsers offline, then back at once on a 600 ms, 40 KB/s line with drops: once each, series unbroken, one request per phone), which also found and fixed the phone bill screen's total bar covering the last line. **Open:** the same test on three real Android phones (with 5.4's app).
* **Added after 5.3, one computer without setup (`installer/`):** `DigitalBahi-Setup.exe` for Windows (NSIS, per user, no administrator rights, shortcuts and uninstaller) and `installer/unix/digitalbahi.sh` for macOS and Linux, sharing one launcher: the computer's own Node.js 22+ and PostgreSQL 16+ when it has them, else both downloaded once and checked against pinned hashes; a database cluster of Digital Bahi's own (own port, this computer only, generated passwords and keys, ICU sorting, migrations at every start); the API serving the built web app (`WEB_DIR`) at http://localhost:8710, open to this computer only because codes are shown on screen. CI (`installer.yml`) builds the .exe and installs, starts, uses, restarts, stops and uninstalls it on a clean Windows machine, and runs the script on Linux. **Open:** signing the .exe (SmartScreen warns until then), and opening a one-computer install to phones on the same Wi-Fi (needs a real OTP gateway and https).
* **5.5 Business intelligence (built):** `GET /insights` and the Insights tab (first on the Reports screen): net sales, gross margin and margin %, cost of goods sold, bills and average bill, customers billed and new, returns and return rate, collections, receivables and days sales outstanding, stock value (from the stock ledger at cost, so at any past date), stock turnover and days of stock — each against the period before or the same dates a year earlier; a 12-month trend; customer, company (brand) and salesperson contribution with share, ABC class and growth, new and lost customers; fast, slow (over 90 days of stock) and dead (no sale in 90 days) stock with the money tied up. The arithmetic (comparison periods, ABC, turnover, DSO) is in `@digitalbahi/core` with its own tests; the API test checks every figure of a two-month business worked out by hand; browser flow `insights-flow.mjs`.
* **5.4 Field sales and the Android app (in progress):** **Beat mode (built):** routes have weekdays and a salesperson, and customers a place on their route (`customers.route_sequence`); `GET /beats/today` gives a salesperson the day's routes (found through the employee linked to them or with their mobile; every route of the day for someone without one), the shops in calling order with what they owe, the last call, the products due to buy again with the usual quantity, and what came of today's call, worked out from the day's bills, orders, payments and visit notes and the new `customer_visits` (checked in, or no order and why; the id made on the phone, so one made offline is kept once; a `/sync/push` kind). With `insights=1` it brings each shop's buying for offline suggestions. `PUT /routes/:id/order` sets the calling order; `GET /reports/beats` sets each salesperson's days against the plan (a call on the plan counts whoever entered it, one off the plan only the salesperson's own). The app's **Beat** screen: today's shops in order with the day's progress, a sheet per shop (bill, an order started with the due products, payment, no order and why, visited, call, WhatsApp), the beat kept on the phone (encrypted) for use without a connection with what was done there counted in; **Routes** (weekdays, salesperson, shops in order) and the **Team report** for managers. Migration `0028_beats`. **Acceptance:** `beats.test.ts` (route order, today's beat, calls from documents and visits, a visit sent twice, insights, the report) and `beat-flow.mjs` in the browser (desk set-up, the phone's beat, an order from the due items, no order, offline beat, suggestions and visit, the team report). **Next in 5.4:** the Android app (Capacitor), Bluetooth thermal printing, the barcode camera and the share sheet; the field test with two salespeople on a real beat.

## K. MVP: the first version a distributor can run their day on

**Goal:** a distributor can stop using their old billing software for daily work: bill customers with correct GST, keep stock right, record purchases, collect money and know who owes what.

### In the MVP

| Area | Included |
|---|---|
| Access | Login (password + OTP), 6 default roles + editable permissions, multiple users, audit log |
| Masters | Customers (with GSTIN validation, credit limit, terms, routes), suppliers, products (HSN, tax category, units, barcodes, rate lists, batch/expiry), godowns, Excel import |
| Sales | **Fast invoice** (desktop + phone), smart suggestions from purchase history (client-side, from synced stats), A4/thermal PDF and print in English/Hindi, WhatsApp/email share, cancel with reversal, payment at sale |
| Purchases | Purchase entry with batches and cost update, purchase returns |
| Inventory | Stock list, stock ledger, adjustments with reasons, low-stock and expiry alerts on the dashboard |
| Money | Customer receipts and supplier payments with allocation, ledgers/statements, outstanding and aging |
| GST | Correct CGST/SGST/UTGST/IGST, dated tax rules, GST register populated from day one, basic GST summary and HSN summary (full return exports in Phase 4) |
| Reports | Dashboard; sales, collection, purchase reports by period, customer, product and company; outstanding; Excel/CSV export |
| Platform | Web app (responsive, installable PWA, online), English + Hindi, daily backups, deployment on a single VPS |

### Deliberately not in the MVP

Sales orders, packing and dispatch · credit/debit notes beyond returns · GSTR-1/3B file exports and filing · e-invoicing and e-way bill · payroll, leave, attendance · expenses · full offline mode and the Android/Windows apps · notifications beyond the dashboard · BI analytics · restore UI (restores done by support).

Why this cut: these are the operations a distributor performs every day and the ones where errors cost money (wrong tax, wrong stock, lost collections). Everything else builds on the same data and can be added module by module without migrating what exists.

## Risks and open questions

| Risk / question | Mitigation / decision needed |
|---|---|
| **GST rates and thresholds in seed and sample data are illustrative** | Verify against current notifications before go-live; maintain as dated data (runbook in [08-gst-architecture.md](08-gst-architecture.md#8-runbook-the-government-changed-a-rule)) |
| E-invoicing needed at launch? (mandatory above the turnover threshold) | If pilot customers are above it, move 6.5 into Phase 4 |
| Single-tenant install vs SaaS first | Architecture supports both; decides hosting, onboarding and pricing |
| Tally data migration | Most distributors run Tally: an import of masters and opening balances (and an export for their CA) is likely a sales requirement |
| Invoice numbering offline | Device series (default) vs "draft until sync": the business's CA should confirm their preference |
| Keyboard shortcuts | Ctrl+P / Ctrl+C conflict with browser Print / Copy; the design uses Alt+P / Alt+C (Ctrl+I and Ctrl+K kept as requested). Confirm with users; a Tally-style F-key preset is planned |
| Hindi copy | Glossary in [05-design-system.md](05-design-system.md#8-localisation) should be reviewed by 3–5 real users (counter staff, salespeople) |

## Definition of done (every milestone)

* Unit tests for business logic in `@digitalbahi/core`; integration tests for every transaction against PostgreSQL; E2E test for the user flow (Playwright).
* English and Hindi strings complete (parity test green).
* Permissions defined and tested; audit entries written for every change to money, stock or access.
* Works at 360 px and 1440 px widths; keyboard reachable; empty/loading/error states present.
* Migrations are additive and backward-compatible; documentation updated.
